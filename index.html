A FIELD GUIDE FOR SMALL PAWS

Make yourself
at hoard.

Survival: each squirrel must deliver five acorns to the wicker basket at the base of its starting tree before the 60-second round ends. Deliveries happen automatically while bringing an acorn to your own basket. Bury extras to recover in later rounds. Drops arrive every three seconds in round one, five seconds in round two, seven seconds in round three, then one second slower each round after that. Players who fall short are eliminated. The other squirrel keeps playing alone until it also misses the goal. The player who survives more rounds wins; elimination in the same round is a draw.

01

Scurry freely. Local human vs human: Red uses WASD and Space to dig; Ash uses arrow keys and Enter. Against AI, either set controls Red: WASD or arrow keys to move, and Space or Enter to dig. Online, either set controls your assigned squirrel. On mobile, use the joystick and Dig button.

02

Keep your stash secret. Carry one acorn at a time. Stand still and dig to bury it. The acorn stays where it was buried, including between rounds, until it is dug up or taken by a crow.

03

Guess. Dig. Recover. Dig with empty paws to search for any buried acorn, including your own.

Arcade: Buried acorns score for the half of the field where they are hidden, regardless of who buried them. Digging one up removes that half’s point. Carry it to your half and bury it again to score for your side. The most acorns buried on your half after three rounds wins.

Survival: Buried acorns are reserves, not points. Dig up a reserve and return it to your own basket to help reach five deliveries that round. Once you have delivered five, bury extra acorns for future rounds as drops become scarce. Falling short of five deliveries eliminates you; the other squirrel can keep playing alone.

04

Between rounds. Loose acorns and acorns in paws clear between rounds; buried stashes remain.

The forest has surprises. Every completed search has a chance of a powerup: three extra acorns, six seconds of speed, a helpful crow, six seconds of slowdown, or a crow stealing from your own patch.
