A FIELD GUIDE FOR SMALL PAWS

Make yourself
at hoard.

01

Scurry freely. Red uses WASD and Space to dig. Ash uses arrows and Enter. Online, either set controls your own squirrel.

02

Keep your stash secret. Carry one acorn at a time. Stand still and dig to bury it. The invisible 40 × 15 grid keeps one acorn per spot, with no marks left behind.

03

Guess. Dig. Recover. Dig with empty paws to search. Find any buried acorn, including your own, carry it home, and bury it again. Its old patch loses a point as soon as it’s dug up.

04

A fresh run each round. Climb down from your tree and return to your starting spot. Loose acorns and acorns in paws clear between rounds; buried stashes remain. Two mirrored 3 × 3 rocks and two 2 × 2 stumps per patch block your path. Five-second breaks separate rounds. Leaves and grass are only scenery.

The forest has surprises. Every completed search has a 10% powerup chance: three extra acorns, six seconds of speed, a helpful crow, six seconds of slowdown, or a crow stealing from your own patch.

Most acorns on your half after round three wins; ties draw. Online matches keep going while menus are open. Both players ready up for a new match; rounds two and three start automatically.
