A FIELD GUIDE FOR SMALL PAWS

Make yourself
at hoard.

01

Scurry freely. Red uses WASD. Ash uses the arrow keys. You can roam anywhere on the board.

02

Find your next acorn. Walk over one to pick it up. You can carry one at a time. One drops at the start, then every 5 seconds.

03

Put down roots. Press Space (Red) or Enter (Ash) to bury your acorn in the highlighted square. Keep still for a short dig.

04

Protect your patch. Every square holds one acorn. A burial scores for the side it’s on: Red’s left half or Ash’s right half.

The round lasts 60 seconds. Most acorns on your half wins. Equal scores mean a draw. Press Esc to pause.
